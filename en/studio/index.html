STUDIO / ABOUTVALUE × X

A content lab.
Expanding what's possible.Valix Lab

VALIX LAB experiments with content and space, testing ideas through audience response and results. One team brings each project from development through production, branding and distribution.

VALIX brings together VALUE and X. We use content to reveal the potential of value yet to be defined.

VALIX LAB is a content lab producing personality-led entertainment and YouTube content, branded and promotional films, and AI and XR short-form dramas. We design formats around each subject and platform, connecting development, production, content branding and distribution in one continuous flow.

We work with specialist partners in art curation, music and mixing, and XR to bring each project the expertise it needs. We extend on-screen stories into physical spaces and online exhibition experiences.
